Privacy Policy – KinderKids

1. Introduction

KinderKids is a fun and educational mobile application designed for children. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe experience for kids.

2. Data Collection

  • KinderKids does NOT collect any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number).
  • No location data or sensitive information is collected.

3. Anonymous Technical Data

  • We may process minimal anonymous technical data to improve app performance.
  • This data cannot be used to identify users.

4. App Features & Content

  • The app provides educational content such as letters, numbers, sounds, and mini-games.
  • All content is available offline and does not require user accounts.

5. Third-Party Services

  • KinderKids may use trusted libraries (animations, media playback) for a better experience.
  • These tools do not collect personal user data.
  • No data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

6. Children’s Privacy

  • KinderKids is designed for children and respects child privacy.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Security

  • Any anonymous data is handled securely.
  • We take reasonable measures to protect user information.

8. App Safety

  • No ads 🚫
  • No in-app purchases 💰
  • 100% kid-friendly environment 🎨
